## 1. TOP PRIORITY TOOL
**Zapier + a unified inbox (Front or Missive)** — tackles email overload first, since it's your #1 drain with a small team.

## 2. IMPLEMENTATION STEPS
- Connect your practice email + payment system (Stripe/Square) into Front for shared, tagged inboxes
- Build Zapier flows: new patient email → auto-categorize → route to right staff member
- Automate payment plan reminders: Zapier + Stripe → auto-send reminder emails/texts on due dates
- Set up a single "app dashboard" (use Zapier or **Make**) to sync patient records across your existing software instead of manual re-entry

## 3. HOURS SAVED PER WEEK
**8–12 hours/week** across your 2-5 person team (mostly reclaimed from email triage and manual payment follow-ups)

## 4. MONTHLY COST
- Zapier (Team plan): ~$69/mo
- Front (Starter, 2-5 seats): ~$95–150/mo
- **Total: ~$165–220/mo**

## 5. 30-DAY ACTION PLAN
**Week 1:** Audit all email types (billing, scheduling, patient Qs) — identify top 3 repetitive ones
**Week 2:** Set up Front + connect Zapier; automate email sorting/routing
**Week 3:** Build payment plan reminder automation (Stripe/Square → Zapier → SMS/email)
**Week 4:** Test software sync automation (Make) between EHR and billing tool; train staff on new workflow

## 6. QUICK WIN (1 Hour)
Set up a **Zapier automation**: When a payment fails or is overdue in Stripe → auto-send a friendly reminder email + Slack notification to staff. Takes 45 minutes, saves 2+ hours/week immediately.\n--------------------------------------------------\n
